Dev block anatomy
Every IDE feature is an independent block: hide it, float it, dock it anywhere, resize it, stack it into tab groups. Open as many instances of a block as you need — Terminal 1, Terminal 2, a second Editor. Layouts persist per project.

Docked
Snapped into the right column or bottom dock. Shares space; resizes neighbors.
Tabbed stack
Drop a block onto another to stack them into one tabbed group. Drag a tab out to split again.
Floating
Glass panel over the page. Free position and size; always on top.
On the rail
Collapsed to an icon on the window edge. One click restores it where it was.
Detached window
The whole deck pops out as its own IDE window — browser and IDE as two apps. Dock back with one click.
Presets
Browsing · Building · Debugging — one keystroke swaps the whole layout. Saved per project.
